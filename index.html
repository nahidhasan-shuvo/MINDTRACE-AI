About the Model

Learn about the AI architecture, dataset, and lead researcher.

🖥️

Model Overview

We employ a multimodal late-fusion deep learning framework (ResNet-18 for drawings, 2D-CNN for MFCC acoustics, and 3D-CNN for kinematic video actions) trained to predict the likelihood of autism spectrum disorder (ASD).

📁

Dataset

Trained on a synchronized pediatric dataset containing matched drawing images, vocal speech recordings, and behavioral kinematic video features across 80% train and 20% test partitions.

📈

Performance

Accuracy: 95% | Precision: 92% | Recall: 78% | Unseen Test Verified
